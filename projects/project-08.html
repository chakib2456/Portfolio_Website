Ghost Echo

Unity C# Platformer Puzzle Design Gameplay Mechanics

Ghost Echo is a puzzle-platformer where you play as a ghost lost in limbo, searching for a way back to your place while escaping demonic creatures and the threat of wandering for eternity.

Your main ability is the Return mechanic, which lets you anchor your current position and teleport back to it at any time. Use this power strategically to explore your surroundings, overcome obstacles, and find your way through the dangers of limbo.

Master your ability, outsmart your pursuers, and find a way home before you become trapped in limbo forever.
