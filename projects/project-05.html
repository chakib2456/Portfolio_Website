Robot Assembly

Unity C# Programming Puzzles Grid-Based Gameplay

Robot Assembly is a programming puzzle game where you control a robot by creating sequences of instructions to solve challenges inside a factory.

Use commands such as Move, Turn, Pick Up, and Drop to navigate the grid, transport energy sources, and activate machinery.

With a limited number of instruction slots, every command counts. Use FOR loops to repeat actions efficiently, overcome obstacles, and find solutions to increasingly challenging puzzles.

Your goal is to deliver energy to the furnace and bring warmth back to the cold factory.
